How it works

Nobody owns it.
Everybody gets paid.

01

Anyone proposes a coin

Name, ticker, image, 0.1 SOL. That's all you give, and it's all you get. No bag, no fees, no keys.

02

The launcher creates it

One public Nodev wallet launches every coin on Raydium LaunchLab. It never holds a token. You can watch it on Solscan.

03

Fees go to holders

Every week, the fees a dev would have kept are paid out in SOL to the coin's holders, pro-rata. Hold the coin, get the pay.

50%25%25%
50% paid to the coin's holders, weekly, in SOL
25% buys back and burns $NODEV
25% runs the launcher and payouts
